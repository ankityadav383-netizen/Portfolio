NABC Mobile App
North American Bengali Conference

A wayfinding and contactless check-in app built to move thousands of attendees through entry and meals without the queues.

Product / UI-UX Design QR Check-in Wayfinding
Project Overview

Celebrating culture, at scale

NABC is an annual conference celebrating Bengali culture and connecting the diaspora community through cultural programs, food and shared events. At the scale of a multi-day conference, getting people through the door and fed without long queues is its own design problem. I took on the role of Product (UI/UX) Designer to build the companion app that attendees, volunteers and coordinators used to make that happen — tailored to a very wide range of ages and comfort with technology.

Objectives

What the App Had to Do

Guide Attendees

An easy, map-based way to find venues, stalls and checkpoints across the event space.

Streamline Entry & Food Distribution

QR codes for quick, contactless entry and food-packet collection instead of manual ticket checks.

Enhance Usability for Families

Let one person manage check-in and food collection for a whole group — kids, parents, grandparents — from a single phone.

Enable Volunteer Assistance

A dedicated scanning mode so volunteers could verify entries and meals quickly at peak times.

Key Feature

User Navigation with Maps

An interactive map let users navigate the event venue with ease. Labelled zones and directions helped attendees locate entry points, food stalls, seating areas and other amenities — without needing to ask around, even on their first visit.

Key Feature

QR Code-Based Access

Every attendee received a unique QR code at ticket purchase. It doubled as both an entry pass, for swift and contactless access at the venue's entry points, and a food voucher, streamlining food-packet collection and cutting wait times on both lines.

Key Feature

Master Control for Multiple Tickets

This addressed the needs of families and groups, particularly those with children or elderly attendees. A single device could manage multiple entries and food collections — a user with several tickets could select individual attendees to check in or collect food on behalf of, without handing out separate phones.

Key Feature

Volunteer Module

Volunteers got a dedicated interface for scanning QR codes at entry points and food stalls, with real-time verification that a code was unique and valid for its assigned entry or meal slot — designed to be picked up during the event itself, with minimal training.

Design Approach

Designing for Every Attendee

User-centric design

Given a diverse user base spanning every age and tech comfort level, the design emphasized simplicity, readability and accessibility above all.

Seamless user flow

From onboarding to QR presentation, every interaction needed minimal taps to reach maps, codes, or the master-control dashboard.

Volunteer interface

Built for clarity and speed, so a new volunteer could get scanning without extensive training.

Challenges & Solutions

Managing Multiple Tickets on One Device

Challenge

Ensuring users could efficiently control multiple entries and food collections from one device without compromising security.

Solution

A master-control dashboard where users could toggle between different attendees' QR codes, reducing complexity while maintaining security checks per person.

Challenges & Solutions

Smooth Volunteer Operations & Diverse Users

Challenge: Volunteer speed

Volunteers needed a straightforward, quick-to-navigate interface, particularly during peak times.

Solution

A minimalistic UI enabling fast access to scanning and real-time validation, cutting attendee wait times.

Challenge: Diverse demographics

The app had to work for tech-savvy young adults and elderly attendees with limited tech experience alike.

Solution

A simplified UI with large, clearly labelled icons and step-by-step prompts made the app accessible to everyone.

Results & Impact

What Changed

Faster entry & food collection

QR-based check-in noticeably cut wait times at both the entrance and the food line.

Self-serve navigation

The interactive map gave attendees an easy way to find their way without asking staff.

Simplified group management

Families and groups could manage multiple attendees with ease via the master-control feature.

Smoother volunteer operations

Volunteers could assist attendees more effectively even during peak crowd times.
