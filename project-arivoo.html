Research Goals

What We Needed to Learn

01 · Understand Teacher Workflow

Map current attendance processes and identify time-consuming steps in classroom routines.

02 · Identify Attendance Pain Points

Discover specific challenges with proxy marking, student recognition, and data accuracy.

03 · Validate Photo-Based Attendance

Test if teachers and students are comfortable with camera-based attendance systems.

04 · Evaluate Accuracy & Privacy

Assess technical accuracy requirements and address privacy concerns from all stakeholders.

Key Insights

What We Discovered

Critical patterns that shaped our AI solution.

Time is Precious

Teachers value every minute of class time. Any solution saving even 3–4 minutes is considered highly valuable.

3–4 min saved

Proxy is a Real Problem

68% of teachers reported awareness of proxy attendance, with larger classes having higher rates.

68% aware

Photo Acceptance

85% of students and teachers were comfortable with photo-based attendance if privacy was ensured.

85% comfortable

Data Insight Need

Admins want attendance trends, not just records. Pattern recognition is key for early intervention.

Pattern analysis

Privacy First

Data storage location and access controls were the #1 concern across all stakeholder groups.

Top concern

Speed Matters

Solution must complete attendance in under 60 seconds to be adopted — any longer feels "too much tech".

<60 seconds
