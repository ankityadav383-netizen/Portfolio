AI Attendance Module for Arivoo SMS

Replacing a slow, easy-to-fake manual roll call with a one-minute, AI-powered face-recognition check-in for classrooms.

AI-Powered Innovation Face Recognition School Management

Overview

Arivoo SMS is a school management platform, and I designed its AI attendance module to take roll call out of teachers' hands entirely. Instead of reading names off a register, a teacher opens the app, points the camera at the class, and the system recognises each student's face and marks them present — in about the time it used to take to say ten names.

The problem

Manual attendance was quietly costing every class real time and real accuracy.

Manual time waste

Teachers spent 5–10 minutes per class taking attendance by hand, breaking up the flow of the lesson.

Proxy attendance

Students could mark a friend present even when they were absent, quietly corrupting the record.

Unknown students

In large classes, teachers couldn't reliably recognise every face, which made proxies easy to get away with.

Poor data tracking

Paper and spreadsheet logs were too messy to analyse, so nobody could spot attendance patterns worth acting on.

The approach

The core interaction had to feel faster than the problem it replaced. A teacher scans the room once; the app detects every face it can, shows a confidence score per match, and only asks for a manual "retake" or "review" on the handful of low-confidence detections — not the whole class. That kept the human in the loop for edge cases without giving up the speed of automation.
