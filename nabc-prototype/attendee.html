Attendee app

Clickable prototype built from the Figma frames.

Try: Explore the event as guest, or type any email and the code 1234 (anything else shows the error). Then the map button on Events, a session, and the dots on the QR pass.

Screens are the Figma frames. The guest button on Sign in is added, using the same tokens (the light-blue secondary button from Your Profile). The Figma flow has no design for the venue map, the schedule view or the registration forms, so those taps do nothing.
