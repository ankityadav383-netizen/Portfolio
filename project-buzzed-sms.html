Problem Statement

The Core Challenges

  1. 01

    Parent Anxiety

    Parents experience constant worry about their children’s safety during transit with no way to track their whereabouts.

  2. 02

    No Real-Time Visibility

    Schools lack live tracking systems, leaving parents and administrators blind to bus locations and delays.

  3. 03

    Manual School Processes

    Communication rely on Bus attendant leave him in very stressed situation.

Research Goals

What We Set Out to Learn

  1. 01

    Understand Parent Concerns

    Identify key anxieties and pain points parents face regarding student safety and communication.

  2. 02

    Improve Bus Attendant Workflow

    Streamline daily tasks like attendance marking, route navigation, and parent communication.

  3. 03

    Streamline School Administration

    Optimize fleet management, and overall operational efficiency for schools.

Key Insights

What We Discovered

Important patterns that shaped design decisions

  • 87%

    Safety Over Features

    87% of parents prioritize safety features over convenience or cost savings.

  • 30s

    Real-Time Expectations

    Parents expect location updates every 30 seconds, not just milestone notifications.

  • 63%

    Trust Through Transparency

    Visible driver/attendant profiles increase parent trust by 63%.

  • 45min

    Manual Process Burden

    Attendants spend 45 minutes daily on paperwork that could be automated.

  • Last Mile

    Route Anxiety

    Parents feel most anxious during the “last mile” when the bus is nearest to school.

  • 92%

    Admin Blind Spots

    92% of schools lack data on route efficiency and on-time performance.

As the user can be not very technoholic. he won’t be able comfortable. Plus there can be a scenario where user don’t use a Smart phone.

Not chosen

It can be a great solution but i got to know from the tech team. Text message have 97% of success rate.

Adding a guardian Profile in Parent app.

If the Parent is allowed to add 2 guardian and attaching their govt. Id & if the attendant has a option to view the profile. He can take the decision very quickly. Quick option to call the parent can be a +.
