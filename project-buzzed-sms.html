BuzzEd Now — Arivoo SMS

Designing a safe and smart school mobility ecosystem, so parents, drivers and school admins always know exactly where every bus is.

Real-Time Tracking Student Safety Smart Management

Overview

BuzzEd Now is the school transport module inside Arivoo SMS. Getting kids to and from school safely is stressful when nobody outside the bus knows where it actually is — so I designed the experience around one question parents and coordinators ask constantly: "where is the bus, right now?"

Three pillars

Every screen in BuzzEd Now was designed to serve one of these three needs, for three very different users on the same trip.

Real-time tracking

A live map lets parents and coordinators follow a bus's exact route and get ahead of delays instead of guessing.

Student safety

Pickup and drop-off events are logged per student, so a parent knows the moment their child boards or gets off.

Smart management

Transport coordinators get one dashboard for the whole fleet — routes, drivers and vehicles — instead of juggling separate tools.
