Virtual Try-on

Clickable prototype of the Senco web plugin, built from the Figma frames.

Start on the product page: click TryOn, accept the notice, then follow the pulsing outline. Add a necklace, a nosepin, then switch to Hand for a bangle and a ring.

Every screen is a Figma frame. Only the paths in the Figma flow are live; other controls say so. The chip states on Feedback and the mobile-number check on Login are added.
