RICE Smart
Study Companion Chatbot

Adding a personalised in-app chatbot to a 100K+ download EdTech platform, so shy students get instant answers without ever raising a hand.

EdTech Conversational UX 4.5 on Play Store
Overview

A leading EdTech app, missing a voice

RICE Smart is an EdTech app with over 100,000 downloads and a 4.5-star rating, built around live doubt-clearing sessions. The objective: introduce a personalised chatbot capable of answering any user query related to the courses they've purchased, offering a seamless, interactive learning experience alongside the live sessions.

The Challenge

Beneficial Sessions, Low Attendance

RICE Smart offers doubt-clearing sessions, yet some users shy away from attending — several are simply too timid to engage in front of the class. The challenge was to build a chatbot that could understand and respond to a wide range of questions across courses and subjects, and open the conversation itself by asking what a student was studying, rather than expecting them to know how to phrase a request for help.

Use Cases

What Students Actually Needed

Post-exam clarity

After a weekly or monthly exam, students want to know exactly which areas to work on.

A sense of standing

Users want to know their position in class to maintain healthy competition, plus performance by individual subject.

A judgement-free way to ask

Most users are introverted and shy — a chatbot lets them ask any question, anytime.

Repeatable, limited questions

A user can ask the same question multiple times, though there's a limited number of questions allowed per month.

Cross-subject answers

One question can have multiple valid answers — e.g. Physics and Chemistry both cover Thermodynamics.

A memory of past questions

Users want to revisit what they asked earlier instead of retyping it.

Possible Solution

My Activity

An activity section to help users track their performance: attendance for classes attended, exams attempted, a leaderboard to see current class standing, and an exam summary giving a brief subject- and topic-wise report of where to focus next.

Possible Solution

Self Quiz

To increase engagement, a self-quiz feature lets a user create a quiz for themselves with AI — just add the subject, topic name and number of questions. The quiz is generated instantly and can be shared with friends, turning revision into something social.

Possible Solution

For the Chatbot

Repeat-question memory

If a user asks a question they've asked before, they're redirected straight to the earlier chat instead of getting a fresh answer.

Course & subject first

The user is asked to specify the course and subject before the bot works on a solution.

A searchable log

A log and history of past questions is maintained, so a user can come back and check earlier answers anytime.
