RICE Smart Study Companion

Adding a personalised in-app chatbot to a 100K+ download EdTech platform, so shy students get instant answers without ever raising a hand.

EdTech Conversational UX 4.5★ on Play Store

Overview

RICE Smart is an EdTech app with over 100,000 downloads and a 4.5-star rating, built around live doubt-clearing sessions. The catch: a lot of students who need help never show up to ask for it. I designed a personalised chatbot that lets any student ask a course-specific question the moment it comes up, instead of waiting for a scheduled session.

The challenge

Doubt-clearing sessions were genuinely useful, but attendance told a different story — many students were simply too shy to ask a question in front of the class. The chatbot needed to understand questions across many courses and subjects, and open the conversation itself by asking what a student was studying, rather than expecting them to know how to phrase a request for help.

What students actually needed

Post-exam clarity

After a weekly or monthly test, students wanted to know exactly which topics to work on next.

A sense of standing

Students wanted to see where they ranked in class and how they were doing subject by subject.

A judgement-free way to ask

Many students are introverted about asking questions out loud, but will type the same question to a chatbot.

A memory of past questions

Students wanted to revisit questions they'd already asked instead of retyping them or losing the answer.

The solution

My Activity — a personal dashboard tracking attendance, exams attempted, a class leaderboard, and a subject-by-subject exam summary showing exactly where to focus next.

Self quiz — students can generate an AI quiz for themselves by naming a subject, topic and question count, then share it with friends, turning revision into something social.

Chat memory — the bot first confirms the course and subject, then keeps a log of past questions; asking something twice re-surfaces the earlier answer instead of starting over, and repeat questions are capped per month to keep usage sustainable.
